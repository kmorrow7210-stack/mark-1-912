Literal structure:  
And immediately **the Spirit** \\\[subject\\\] **him** \\\[object\\\] **drives out** \\\[verb\\\] **into the wilderness** \\\[destination\\\].  
Smooth translation:  
“And immediately the Spirit drives him out into the wilderness.”  
Two important observations:

* **ἐκβάλλει** is a vivid historical present: Mark narrates a past event as though it is happening before us. The verb is forceful—“drives out” or “impels”—stronger than simply “leads.”  
* **τὸ πνεῦμα** refers contextually to the Spirit who descended upon Jesus in verse 10\. Jesus does not wander accidentally into the wilderness; the Spirit sends him there.
